Frozen joint-reference cross-dataset expansion - 7 November 2026

Design locked before new inference; not a preregistration or a blinded study.
The original 14-section audit remains unchanged and is not used for tuning.
No model fitting, feature selection, score-weight change or threshold adaptation.

Sources and scope
GSE210616: all eight locally available TNBC sections, four explicit patient IDs
in author GEO titles. This is a local subset of the 43-record source series.
GSE300676: all eight LUAD FFPE sections, four source cases with two sections each.
GSE175540: all 24 RCC sections, 12 FFPE and 12 frozen. Patient identifiers are
not verified; do not treat section names as independently verified patients.
GSE210041: both locally available RCC sections. Names T1/T7 are specimen labels;
patient uniqueness is not assumed for inferential statistics.

All four studies were excluded from joint-reference fitting/calibration/testing.
They were previously examined with legacy models. Cross-study patient aliases
cannot be fully ruled out. The same three cancer categories occurred in joint
training, so this tests new source studies and measurement platforms, not unseen
cancer categories. Counts are sections and source studies, not independent trials.

Inputs and frozen state
Read original full 10x H5 gene IDs and counts. Do not use legacy reduced panels.
Reuse the frozen 2,000 features, 31,738-gene denominator, three checkpoint weights,
training centroids and synthetic-mixture calibration. Hash and replay checks are
required. Map only frozen unambiguous gene aliases. Record missing genes and count
mass; exclude zero-common-count spots. The existing 90% model-feature coverage
gate separates coverage-eligible from feature-limited transport diagnostics.

Endpoints
Every section: raw and scaled score distributions, saturation, and actual
retention under original nominal 70%, 80% and 90% thresholds. These are transport
diagnostics, not accuracy, exact composition error or OOD-detection AUROC.
RCC GSE175540: only explicit TLS/NO_TLS annotations are binary labels. T_AGG,
unspecified and missing annotations are not relabelled as binary truth.
Use B/Plasma fraction to rank author TLS structure as the primary biology
plausibility control; summed immune fraction is secondary. Neither yields
exact cell-composition truth. TLS is not labelled a mapping failure.
Report TLS and non-TLS retention, class counts and B/Plasma-TLS AUROC before
and after original-threshold and retrospective within-section 80% retention.
One-class retained subsets yield missing AUROC, never a fabricated zero or one.

Baselines
Entropy, novelty, disagreement and the existing 0.5/0.3/0.2 fixed fusion.
For TLS retention only, add one-minus-maximum-probability and the B/Plasma
binary margin as composition-derived score controls. All use the same frozen
ensemble predictions. No mature deconvolution rerun is claimed in this batch.
Ties use fixed barcode hashes independent of labels, as in the preceding audit.

Statistics
Breast: first average sections within each of the four explicit source patients.
Lung: first average paired sections within the four source cases.
Within-source patient/case bootstrap intervals use 5,000 draws. They do not
establish independence between source studies or yield clinical uncertainty.
RCC cohorts: section-level descriptive summaries only while identities remain
unresolved. No population confidence intervals or spot-pooled significance.
Stratify FFPE/frozen preparations. Do not pool unlike biological endpoints or
declare general accuracy based on unlabeled cohorts. No superiority hypothesis
is added after observing a favorable section.

Excluded source
GSE283052 CRC matrices were previously assigned a generic reference feature
list based on matching row counts. Original gene-row correspondence has not
been independently verified. Exclude them from this batch rather than claim
additional reliable external validation. No new CRC rescue is claimed.

Storage and reporting
Inputs remain read-only. D: reports a filesystem warning during the local run;
small predictions and reports use a separate recovery workspace. No raw dataset
copies or new large downloads are needed. Publish code and aggregate tables only.
Keep all failures and negative outcomes. Original submission files are untouched.
