Spatial coverage calibration and source-held-out policy transfer
Locked 7 November 2026 before new-source model inference.

Motivation and exposure
The earlier 56-section frozen audit exposed severe coverage shift and TLS loss.
This is an exploratory development experiment motivated by those results, not
a preregistered or blinded confirmation. Preserve every previous result.
All new test sources have legacy-model exposure. Their frozen joint-model
predictions and responses to the policies below have not been inspected when
this protocol is locked. Cross-study donor aliases remain unresolved.

Calibration
Use GSE210616 (8 frozen TNBC sections / 4 explicit patients) for frozen tissue,
and GSE300676 (8 FFPE LUAD sections / 4 source cases) for FFPE diagnostics.
Only unlabeled scores and predicted composition from the previous frozen run
may enter calibration. No pathology or TLS labels, test-source scores or test
metadata beyond format/preparation/identity can determine thresholds.
Weights give each patient/case equal mass, then each section within patient
equal mass, then each spot within section equal mass.

Policies, specified before inference
Nominal retention 80%, selected before this experiment.
1. Original synthetic-mixture threshold (the four original uncertainty scores).
2. Spatial pooled weighted 80th percentile, separately for frozen and FFPE.
3. Spatial predicted-dominant-class conditional weighted 80th percentile.
Conditional bins require >=100 calibration spots and >=3 calibration patients;
otherwise use the pre-specified pooled fallback. The seven original predicted
classes define bins. No TLS, pathology or outcome label enters this rule.
4. Label-blind hash-based 80% retention as a null control.

Scores
Original entropy, clipped novelty, clipped disagreement, fixed 0.5/0.3/0.2
fusion; add raw novelty, raw disagreement, and one-minus-maximum-probability
controls. Do not refit the mapper, centroids, feature set, score weights or
original normalization. Clipped-score saturation is explicitly reported.
At a weighted quantile tie, apply a fixed sample+barcode hash with an acceptance
fraction fixed from calibration tied mass. Restoring retention by breaking
constant-score ties is random selection, not restored risk discrimination.

Validity and statistical units
Below 90% frozen model-feature coverage, all automatic interpretation is disabled:
review-only status. Report hypothetical threshold behavior separately. Meeting
coverage alone never certifies accuracy. Do not extrapolate calibration across
an unrecognized preparation. Breast/lung calibration diagnostics are patient/case
balanced. New test summaries are descriptive section means unless source patient
identities are verified. Do not pool spots to inflate independent sample size.
The gastric GC6/GC6-PM title pair is not by itself verified donor linkage.
RCC TLS source patient IDs are unresolved; no patient population CI is claimed.
No conformal risk, distribution-free coverage, or clinical guarantee is claimed.
